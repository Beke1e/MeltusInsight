Project information

Meltus Insight

A diabetes patient-management and prediction platform.

Application functionalities

Patient registration and management: Create, view, edit, search, and delete patient records with automatically generated registration dates and Patient IDs.

Diabetes prediction: Select a Patient ID and submit the clinical model inputs to generate and save a diabetes prediction.

Prediction result management: View, edit, and delete prediction records, with every result linked to its Patient ID.

Prediction export and filtering: Filter prediction records by Patient ID, outcome, and date range, then export prediction inputs, outcomes, and timestamps without exporting general patient records.

Research background

The diabetes mellitus dataset from Debark General Hospital was prepared through missing-value handling, outlier removal, categorical encoding, normalization, class-balancing augmentation, feature selection, and dimensionality reduction. The model uses a multilayer perceptron with dense layers, dropout, and an attention-inspired feature-learning design.
